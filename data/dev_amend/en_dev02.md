Article 1. Amending and supplementing a number of articles of the Civil Code No. 91/2015/QH13

1. Clause 1 of Article 621 is amended as follows:

“1. The following persons are disqualified from receiving an estate under the provisions of law:

a) A person who has been convicted of an act of intentionally infringing upon the life or health of the estate leaver, or of seriously mistreating or torturing the estate leaver, or of seriously infringing upon the honor or dignity of the estate leaver;

b) A person who has seriously breached the duty to support and care for the estate leaver;

c) A person who has been convicted of an act of intentionally infringing upon the life of another heir in order to receive a part or the whole of the estate to which such heir is entitled;

d) A person who, by deception, coercion or obstruction, causes the estate leaver to make, forge, alter or revoke a will, or who conceals a will, in order to receive a part or the whole of the estate against the will of the estate leaver.”

Article 2. Effect

This Law takes effect on July 1, 2027.
