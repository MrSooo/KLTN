# Inheritance (Articles 609 – 662\)

### Article 609\. Inheritance right

Every individual may make a will to dispose of his/her assets; leave his/her assets to his/her heirs at law; and inherit estates under a will or at law.

An heir which is not a natural person may inherit an estate under a will.

### Article 610\. Equality in inheritance rights of individuals

Every individual is equal in the right to leave his/her assets to others and to inherit estates under a will or at law.

### Article 611\. Time and place for opening of an inheritance

**1\.** The time for opening of an inheritance is the time when the person owning the assets dies. Where a court declares a person dead, the time for opening of the inheritance is the date determined in clause 2 of Article 71 of this Code.

**2\.** The place for opening of an inheritance is the last place of residence of the estate leaver; if the last place of residence cannot be determined, the place for opening of the inheritance is the place where the whole or the majority of the estate is located.

### Article 612\. Estate

An estate comprises the separate property of the deceased and the share of the property of the deceased in property held in common with others.

### Article 613\. Heirs

A natural person who is an heir must be alive at the time of opening of the inheritance, or be born and alive after the time of opening of the inheritance but conceived before the death of the estate leaver. Where an heir under a will is not a natural person, it must exist at the time of opening of the inheritance.

### Article 614\. Time when rights and obligations of heirs arise

From the time of opening of the inheritance, the heirs have the rights and obligations with respect to the property left by the deceased.

### Article 615\. Performance of property obligations left by the deceased

**1\.** The persons who receive the inheritance are responsible for performing the property obligations left by the deceased within the scope of the estate, except where otherwise agreed.

**2\.** Where the estate has not yet been distributed, the property obligations left by the deceased shall be performed by the administrator of the estate in accordance with the agreement of the heirs, within the scope of the estate left by the deceased.

**3\.** Where the estate has been distributed, each heir shall perform the property obligations left by the deceased in proportion to, but not exceeding, the property which he/she has received, except where otherwise agreed.

**4\.** Where an heir which is not a natural person receives an estate under a will, it must also perform the property obligations left by the deceased in the same manner as a natural heir.

### Article 616\. Administrator of the estate

**1\.** The administrator of the estate is the person designated in the will or appointed by agreement of the heirs.

**2\.** Where the will does not designate an administrator and the heirs have not yet appointed one, the person who is managing, using and administering the estate shall continue to administer the estate until the heirs appoint an administrator of the estate.

**3\.** Where the heirs have not yet been identified and there is no administrator of the estate in accordance with clauses 1 and 2 of this Article, the estate shall be administered by a competent state agency.

### Article 617\. Obligations of the administrator of the estate

**1\.** The administrator of the estate specified in clauses 1 and 3 of Article 616 of this Code has the following obligations:

- **a)** To make a list of the estate; to recover property of the estate held by others, unless otherwise provided by law;  
- **b)** To preserve the estate; not to sell, exchange, donate, pledge, mortgage or otherwise dispose of the property without the written consent of the heirs;  
- **c)** To notify the heirs of the condition of the estate;  
- **d)** To compensate for damage caused by a breach of his/her obligations;  
- **đ)** To hand over the estate at the request of the heirs.

**2\.** The person who is managing, using and administering the estate specified in clause 2 of Article 616 of this Code has the following obligations:

- **a)** To preserve the estate; not to sell, exchange, donate, pledge, mortgage or otherwise dispose of the property;  
- **b)** To notify the heirs of the estate;  
- **c)** To compensate for damage caused by a breach of his/her obligations;  
- **d)** To hand over the estate in accordance with the agreement in the contract with the estate leaver or at the request of the heirs.

### Article 618\. Rights of the administrator of the estate

**1\.** The administrator of the estate specified in clauses 1 and 3 of Article 616 of this Code has the following rights:

- **a)** To represent the heirs in relations with third parties with respect to the estate;  
- **b)** To receive remuneration as agreed with the heirs;  
- **c)** To be reimbursed for the costs of preserving the estate.

**2\.** The person who is managing, using and administering the estate specified in clause 2 of Article 616 of this Code has the following rights:

- **a)** To continue using the estate in accordance with the agreement in the contract with the estate leaver or with the consent of the heirs;  
- **b)** To receive remuneration as agreed with the heirs;  
- **c)** To be reimbursed for the costs of preserving the estate.

**3\.** Where no agreement with the heirs is reached on the level of remuneration, the administrator of the estate shall be entitled to a reasonable remuneration.

### Article 619\. Inheritance by persons who are each other's heirs but die at the same time

Where persons who are each other's heirs die at the same time or are deemed to have died at the same time because it is impossible to determine who died first, they may not inherit each other's estates, and the estate of each person shall be distributed to his/her own heirs, except for inheritance by substitute under Article 652 of this Code.

### Article 620\. Disclaimer of an estate

**1\.** An heir may disclaim an estate, unless the disclaimer is for the purpose of evading the performance of his/her property obligations toward other persons.

**2\.** A disclaimer of an estate must be made in writing and sent to the administrator of the estate, the other heirs and the person assigned to distribute the estate.

**3\.** A disclaimer of an estate must be made before the time of distribution of the estate.

### Article 621\. Persons disqualified from receiving an estate

**1\.** The following persons are disqualified from receiving an estate:

- **a)** A person who has been convicted of an act of intentionally infringing upon the life or health of the estate leaver, or of seriously mistreating or torturing the estate leaver, or of seriously infringing upon the honor or dignity of the estate leaver;  
- **b)** A person who has seriously breached the duty to support the estate leaver;  
- **c)** A person who has been convicted of an act of intentionally infringing upon the life of another heir in order to receive a part or the whole of the estate to which such heir is entitled;  
- **d)** A person who, by deception, coercion or obstruction, causes the estate leaver to make, forge, alter or revoke a will, or who conceals a will, in order to receive a part or the whole of the estate against the will of the estate leaver.

**2\.** Persons specified in clause 1 of this Article may still receive the estate if the estate leaver knew of their acts but still allowed them to receive the estate under the will.

### Article 622\. Property without heirs

Where there are no heirs under a will or at law, or where there are heirs but they are disqualified from receiving the estate or disclaim the estate, the remaining property, after the performance of property obligations, shall belong to the State.

### Article 623\. Statute of limitations for inheritance

**1\.** The statute of limitations for inheritance is as follows:

- **a)** The estate belongs to the person in possession under Article 236 of this Code;  
- **b)** The estate belongs to the State if there is no person in possession as specified in point a of this clause.

**2\.** The statute of limitations for an heir to request confirmation of his/her inheritance right or rejection of another person's inheritance right is 10 years from the time of opening of the inheritance.

**3\.** The statute of limitations for requesting an heir to perform the property obligations left by the deceased is 03 years from the time of opening of the inheritance.

### Article 624\. Will

A will is the manifestation of the will of an individual to transfer his/her assets to other persons after death.

### Article 625\. Testators

**1\.** An adult who satisfies the conditions specified in point a of clause 1 of Article 630 of this Code may make a will to dispose of his/her assets.

**2\.** A person aged between fifteen and eighteen may make a will with the consent of his/her parents or guardian.

### Article 626\. Rights of a testator

A testator has the following rights:

**1\.** Designate heirs and deprive an heir of the right to receive the estate.

**2\.** Determine the portion of the estate to be received under the will by each heir.

**3\.** Reserve a part of his/her property in the estate as a gift or for worship.

**4\.** Assign obligations to heirs.

**5\.** Designate the custodian of the will, the administrator of the estate and the distributor of the estate.

### Article 627\. Form of wills

A will must be made in writing; where it is impossible to make a will in writing, an oral will may be made.

### Article 628\. Written wills

A written will may be one of the following types:

**1\.** A written will without witnesses.

**2\.** A written will with witnesses.

**3\.** A written will which is notarized.

**4\.** A written will which is certified.

### Article 629\. Oral wills

**1\.** Where a person's life is threatened by death and he/she cannot make a written will, he/she may make an oral will.

**2\.** Where, after 03 months from the time of making the oral will, the testator is still alive and lucid, the oral will shall be automatically revoked.

### Article 630\. Lawful wills

**1\.** A will is lawful if it satisfies all of the following conditions:

- **a)** The testator is lucid when making the will; and is not deceived, threatened or coerced;  
- **b)** The contents of the will are not contrary to the law or social ethics; and the form of the will is not contrary to the provisions of law.

**2\.** A will made by a person aged between fifteen and eighteen must be made in writing and consented to by his/her parents or guardian.

**3\.** A will made by a physically limited or illiterate person must be written by a witness and notarized or certified.

**4\.** A written will which is not notarized or certified shall be deemed lawful only if it satisfies the conditions specified in clause 1 of this Article.

**5\.** An oral will shall be deemed lawful if the testator expresses his/her final wish before at least two witnesses, and the witnesses immediately record and jointly sign or fingerprint it. Within 05 working days from the date the testator expresses his/her final wish, the will must be certified by a notary or a competent authority as to the signatures or fingerprints of the witnesses.

### Article 631\. Contents of a will

**1\.** A will has the following main contents:

- **a)** The day, month and year when the will is made;  
- **b)** The full name and place of residence of the testator;  
- **c)** The full names of the persons, agencies or organizations entitled to receive the estate;  
- **d)** The estate to be left and its location.

**2\.** In addition to the contents specified in clause 1 of this Article, a will may have other contents.

**3\.** A will may not be written in abbreviation or symbol; where a will has several pages, each page must be numbered and bear the signature or fingerprint of the testator.

### Article 632\. Witnesses to the making of wills

Every person may act as a witness to the making of a will, except for the following persons:

**1\.** An heir of the testator under the will or at law.

**2\.** A person whose property rights or obligations are related to the contents of the will.

**3\.** A minor, a legally incapacitated person, or a person with cognition or behavior control difficulties.

### Article 633\. Written wills without witnesses

The testator must write and sign the will by himself/herself.

The making of a written will without witnesses must comply with Article 631 of this Code.

### Article 634\. Written wills with witnesses

Where the testator cannot write the will by himself/herself, he/she may type it or ask another person to write or type it, but there must be at least two witnesses. The testator must sign or fingerprint the will in the presence of the witnesses, who shall certify the signature or fingerprint and sign the will.

The making of a written will with witnesses must comply with Articles 631 and 632 of this Code.

### Article 635\. Notarized or certified wills

A testator may request the notarization or certification of his/her will.

### Article 636\. Procedures for making wills at notarial practice organizations or commune-level People's Committees

The making of a will at a notarial practice organization or a commune-level People's Committee must comply with the following procedures:

**1\.** The testator declares the contents of the will before the notary or the person competent to certify of the commune-level People's Committee, who shall record the declared contents. The testator signs or fingerprints the will after confirming that it has been recorded accurately. The notary or certifying person signs the will.

**2\.** Where the testator cannot read, hear, sign or fingerprint, a witness is required, who must sign in confirmation before the notary or certifying person.

### Article 637\. Persons prohibited from notarizing or certifying wills

A notary or a competent person of a commune-level People's Committee may not notarize or certify a will in the following cases:

**1\.** He/she is an heir of the testator under the will or at law.

**2\.** His/her father, mother, wife, husband or child is an heir of the testator under the will or at law.

**3\.** He/she has property rights or obligations related to the contents of the will.

### Article 638\. Written wills having the same value as notarized or certified wills

A written will of a person who cannot request notarization or certification due to circumstances such as military service or a voyage, and who is certified by his/her unit as being lucid when making the will, has the same value as a notarized or certified will.

### Article 639\. Amendment, supplement, replacement or revocation of wills

A testator may amend, supplement, replace or revoke his/her will at any time.

### Article 640\. Joint wills of husband and wife

A husband and wife may make a joint will to dispose of their common property.

Where the marriage is dissolved or the will is revoked by one spouse, the joint will, or the part of the joint will related to the common property, ceases to have effect in accordance with law.

### Article 641\. Custody of wills

The custodian of a will shall keep the will safe, and where a will is lost or damaged must immediately notify the testator.

### Article 642\. Lost or damaged wills

**1\.** Where a will is lost or damaged to the extent that it cannot fully manifest the will of the testator and there is no evidence of the testator's genuine wish, it shall be deemed that there is no will and the provisions of law on inheritance at law shall apply.

**2\.** Where the estate has not yet been distributed and the will is found, the estate shall be distributed according to the will.

**3\.** Where the estate has already been distributed and the will is found within the statute of limitations for requesting distribution of the estate, the estate shall be re-distributed according to the will upon the request of the heirs under the will.

### Article 643\. Effect of wills

**1\.** A will takes effect from the time of opening of the inheritance.

**2\.** A will shall be wholly or partly ineffective in the following cases:

- **a)** An heir under the will dies before or at the same time as the testator;  
- **b)** An agency or organization designated as an heir no longer exists at the time of opening of the inheritance.

Where several heirs are designated, and one dies before or at the same time as the testator, or one of the designated agencies or organizations no longer exists, only the part of the will related to that person, agency or organization shall be ineffective.

**3\.** A will shall be ineffective where the estate left to the heirs no longer exists at the time of opening of the inheritance; where only part of the estate remains, the part of the will relating to the remaining part shall remain effective.

**4\.** Where a will has an unlawful part which does not affect the effectiveness of the remaining parts, only that part shall be ineffective.

**5\.** Where a person leaves several wills in respect of the same property, only the latest will shall be effective.

### Article 644\. Heirs who are not dependent on the contents of a will

**1\.** The following persons may still receive their shares of an estate equal to two-thirds of the share of an heir at law if the estate is distributed according to law, in cases where they are not permitted by the testator to receive the estate, or are permitted to receive only a share which is less than two thirds of such amount:

- **a)** Minor children, fathers, mothers, wives and husbands;  
- **b)** Adult children who are incapable of working.

**2\.** Clause 1 of this Article shall not apply to persons who have disclaimed the estate under Article 620, or to persons who are disqualified from receiving the estate under clause 1 of Article 621 of this Code.

### Article 645\. Estate used for worship

**1\.** Where a testator leaves a part of the estate to be used for worship, that part shall not be divided among the heirs and shall be handed over to the person designated in the will to manage it for worship purposes.

Where the testator does not designate a person to manage the estate used for worship, the heirs shall appoint such a person.

Where all heirs under the will have died, the part of the estate used for worship shall belong to the person among the heirs at law who is lawfully managing the estate.

**2\.** Where the whole estate of the deceased is insufficient to perform his/her property obligations, no part of the estate may be reserved for worship.

### Article 646\. Testamentary gift

**1\.** A testamentary gift is a part of the estate which the testator reserves to give to another person. The gift must be clearly stated in the will.

**2\.** A natural person who is a testamentary giftee must be alive at the time of opening of the inheritance, or be born and alive after the time of opening of the inheritance but conceived before the death of the estate leaver. Where the giftee is not a natural person, it must exist at the time of opening of the inheritance.

**3\.** The giftee is not liable for the property obligations related to the gifted part, unless the whole estate is insufficient to perform the property obligations of the testator, in which case the gifted part shall also be used to perform the remaining obligations.

### Article 647\. Publication of wills

**1\.** Where a written will is kept at a notarial practice organization, the notary shall publish the will.

**2\.** Where the testator designates a person to publish the will, that person shall publish it; where no one is designated or the designated person refuses, the remaining heirs shall agree to appoint a person to publish the will.

**3\.** After the time of opening of the inheritance, the publisher shall send a copy of the will to all persons related to the contents of the will.

**4\.** A person who receives a copy of a will may request a comparison with the original.

**5\.** Where a will is made in a foreign language, it must be translated into Vietnamese and notarized or certified.

### Article 648\. Interpretation of the contents of wills

Where the contents of a will are unclear and may be understood in different ways, the heirs under the will shall together interpret the contents based on the testator's genuine wish, taking into account his/her relationship with the heirs under the will. Where they cannot agree on the interpretation, they may request the court to settle the matter.

Where part of the will cannot be interpreted but the remaining parts are unaffected, only the uninterpretable part shall be ineffective.

## Chapter XXIII – INHERITANCE AT LAW

### Article 649\. Inheritance at law

Inheritance at law is inheritance according to the order of priority of heirs and the conditions and order of inheritance provided for by law.

### Article 650\. Cases of inheritance at law

**1\.** Inheritance at law shall apply in the following cases:

- **a)** There is no will;  
- **b)** The will is unlawful;  
- **c)** The heirs under the will have died before or at the same time as the testator; the agencies or organizations which are entitled to inherit under the will no longer exist at the time of opening of the inheritance;  
- **d)** The persons designated as heirs under the will are disqualified from receiving the estate or have disclaimed the estate.

**2\.** Inheritance at law shall also apply to the following parts of an estate:

- **a)** The part of the estate which has not been disposed of by the will;  
- **b)** The part of the estate related to the part of the will which is legally invalid;  
- **c)** The part of the estate related to the heirs under the will who are disqualified from receiving the estate, who have disclaimed the estate, or who have died before or at the same time as the testator; and to agencies or organizations which are entitled to inherit under the will but no longer exist at the time of opening of the inheritance.

### Article 651\. Heirs at law

**1\.** Heirs at law are determined in the following order of priority:

- **a)** First level of heirs: wives, husbands, natural fathers, natural mothers, adoptive fathers, adoptive mothers, natural children and adopted children of the deceased;  
- **b)** Second level of heirs: paternal grandparents and maternal grandparents of the deceased; natural brothers and natural sisters of the deceased; natural grandchildren of the deceased of whom the deceased is a paternal grandparent or maternal grandparent;  
- **c)** Third level of heirs: paternal great-grandparents and maternal great-grandparents of the deceased; natural uncles and natural aunts of the deceased; natural grandchildren of the deceased of whom the deceased is a paternal great-grandparent or maternal great-grandparent.

**2\.** Heirs at the same level shall receive equal shares of the estate.

**3\.** Heirs at a lower level may only receive the estate if no one exists at a higher level to inherit due to their death, disqualification from receiving the estate, deprivation of the right to receive the estate, or disclaimer of the estate.

### Article 652\. Inheritance by substitution

Where a child of the estate leaver dies before or at the same time as the estate leaver, the grandchild shall receive the part of the estate which his/her father or mother would have received if alive; where the grandchild has also died before or at the same time as the estate leaver, the great-grandchild shall receive the part of the estate which his/her father or mother would have received if alive.

### Article 653\. Inheritance relations between adopted children and adoptive parents and biological parents

Adopted children and adoptive parents may inherit each other's estates, and may also inherit estates under Articles 651 and 652 of this Code.

### Article 654\. Inheritance relations between stepchildren and stepparents

Where stepchildren and stepparents have cared for and supported each other as parent and child, they may inherit each other's estates, and may also inherit estates under Articles 652 and 653 of this Code.

### Article 655\. Inheritance where spouses have already divided their common property, are requesting divorce, or have remarried

**1\.** Where spouses have divided their common property during the marriage and one of them later dies, the survivor may still inherit the estate.

**2\.** Where spouses have requested a divorce which has not yet been granted, or has been granted by a judgment or decision which has not yet taken legal effect, and one of them dies, the survivor may still inherit the estate.

**3\.** A person who is the wife or husband of another person at the time of the latter's death may still inherit the estate even if he/she has subsequently remarried.

## Chapter XXIV – SETTLEMENT AND DISTRIBUTION OF ESTATES

### Article 656\. Meeting of heirs

**1\.** After being notified of the opening of the inheritance or the publication of the will, the heirs may meet to agree on the following matters:

- **a)** Appointing the administrator of the estate and the distributor of the estate, and determining their rights and obligations, if the estate leaver has not designated them in the will;  
- **b)** The mode of distribution of the estate.

**2\.** All agreements of the heirs must be made in writing.

### Article 657\. Distributor of the estate

**1\.** The distributor of the estate may also be the administrator of the estate designated in the will or appointed by agreement of the heirs.

**2\.** The distributor of the estate must distribute the estate in accordance with the will or the agreement of the heirs at law.

**3\.** The distributor of the estate is entitled to remuneration if so permitted by the estate leaver in the will or agreed by the heirs.

### Article 658\. Order of priority for payment

Property obligations and costs related to inheritance shall be paid in the following order of priority:

**1\.** Reasonable funeral costs in accordance with custom.

**2\.** Unpaid support payments.

**3\.** Costs of preserving the estate.

**4\.** Allowances for dependents.

**5\.** Wages for labor.

**6\.** Damage compensation.

**7\.** Taxes and other amounts payable to the state budget.

**8\.** Other debts owed to natural or legal persons.

**9\.** Fines.

**10\.** Other costs.

### Article 659\. Distribution of estates under wills

**1\.** The estate shall be distributed according to the will of the testator; where the will does not clearly determine the share of each heir, the estate shall be divided equally among the persons designated in the will, except where otherwise agreed.

**2\.** Where the will determines the distribution of the estate in kind, each heir shall receive the kind together with the fruits and income derived from it, or shall bear the decreased value of the kind up to the time of distribution of the estate; where the kind has been destroyed through the fault of another person, the heir may request compensation for damage.

**3\.** Where the will only determines the distribution of the estate according to a proportion of the total value, that proportion shall be calculated on the value of the estate remaining at the time of distribution.

### Article 660\. Distribution of estates at law

**1\.** When distributing the estate, where an heir at the same level has been conceived but not yet born, a part of the estate equal to the share of another heir shall be reserved so that it can be received if the heir is born alive; where he/she dies before birth, that part shall belong to the other heirs.

**2\.** The heirs may request the distribution of the estate in kind; where the estate cannot be equally divided in kind, the heirs may agree on the valuation of the kind and on the person receiving it; where no agreement is reached, the kind shall be sold and distributed.

### Article 661\. Restriction on distribution of estates

Where, according to the will of the testator or the agreement of all heirs, the estate may only be distributed after a certain period, the estate shall be distributed only after that period has expired.

Where a request to distribute the estate would seriously affect the life of the surviving wife or husband and the family, the survivor may request a court to determine the part of the estate to which the heirs are entitled but not to distribute the estate for a certain period. This period shall not exceed 03 years from the time of opening of the inheritance. Where, after 03 years, the survivor proves that the distribution still seriously affects the life of the family, he/she may request the court to extend the period once, but not for more than 03 years.

### Article 662\. Distribution of estates where a new heir appears or an heir is deprived of the right to inherit

**1\.** Where the estate has been distributed but a new heir appears, the estate shall not be re-distributed in kind, but the heirs who have received the estate must pay the new heir an amount corresponding to his/her share at the time of distribution in proportion to the share they have received, except where otherwise agreed.

**2\.** Where the estate has been distributed but an heir is deprived of the right to inherit, that heir must return the estate or pay an amount equivalent to the value of the estate he/she has received at the time of distribution to the other heirs, except where otherwise agreed.
