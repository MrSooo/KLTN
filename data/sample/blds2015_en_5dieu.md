### Article 621\. Persons disqualified from receiving an estate

**1\.** The following persons are disqualified from receiving an estate:

- **a)** A person who has been convicted of an act of intentionally infringing upon the life or health of the estate leaver, or of seriously mistreating or torturing the estate leaver, or of seriously infringing upon the honor or dignity of the estate leaver;  
- **b)** A person who has seriously breached the duty to support the estate leaver;  
- **c)** A person who has been convicted of an act of intentionally infringing upon the life of another heir in order to receive a part or the whole of the estate to which such heir is entitled;  
- **d)** A person who, by deception, coercion or obstruction, causes the estate leaver to make, forge, alter or revoke a will, or who conceals a will, in order to receive a part or the whole of the estate against the will of the estate leaver.

**2\.** Persons specified in clause 1 of this Article may still receive the estate if the estate leaver knew of their acts but still allowed them to receive the estate under the will.

### Article 626\. Rights of a testator

A testator has the following rights:

**1\.** Designate heirs and deprive an heir of the right to receive the estate.

**2\.** Determine the portion of the estate to be received under the will by each heir.

**3\.** Reserve a part of his/her property in the estate as a gift or for worship.

**4\.** Assign obligations to heirs.

**5\.** Designate the custodian of the will, the administrator of the estate and the distributor of the estate.

### Article 644\. Heirs who are not dependent on the contents of a will

**1\.** The following persons may still receive their shares of an estate equal to two-thirds of the share of an heir at law if the estate is distributed according to law, in cases where they are not permitted by the testator to receive the estate, or are permitted to receive only a share which is less than two thirds of such amount:

- **a)** Minor children, fathers, mothers, wives and husbands;  
- **b)** Adult children who are incapable of working.

**2\.** Clause 1 of this Article shall not apply to persons who have disclaimed the estate under Article 620, or to persons who are disqualified from receiving the estate under clause 1 of Article 621 of this Code.

### Article 650\. Cases of inheritance at law

**1\.** Inheritance at law shall apply in the following cases:

- **a)** There is no will;  
- **b)** The will is unlawful;  
- **c)** The heirs under the will have died before or at the same time as the testator; the agencies or organizations which are entitled to inherit under the will no longer exist at the time of opening of the inheritance;  
- **d)** The persons designated as heirs under the will are disqualified from receiving the estate or have disclaimed the estate.

**2\.** Inheritance at law shall also apply to the following parts of an estate:

- **a)** The part of the estate which has not been disposed of by the will;  
- **b)** The part of the estate related to the part of the will which is legally invalid;  
- **c)** The part of the estate related to the heirs under the will who are disqualified from receiving the estate, who have disclaimed the estate, or who have died before or at the same time as the testator; and to agencies or organizations which are entitled to inherit under the will but no longer exist at the time of opening of the inheritance.

### Article 651\. Heirs at law

**1\.** Heirs at law are determined in the following order of priority:

- **a)** First level of heirs: wives, husbands, natural fathers, natural mothers, adoptive fathers, adoptive mothers, natural children and adopted children of the deceased;  
- **b)** Second level of heirs: paternal grandparents and maternal grandparents of the deceased; natural brothers and natural sisters of the deceased; natural grandchildren of the deceased of whom the deceased is a paternal grandparent or maternal grandparent;  
- **c)** Third level of heirs: paternal great-grandparents and maternal great-grandparents of the deceased; natural uncles and natural aunts of the deceased; natural grandchildren of the deceased of whom the deceased is a paternal great-grandparent or maternal great-grandparent.

**2\.** Heirs at the same level shall receive equal shares of the estate.

**3\.** Heirs at a lower level may only receive the estate if no one exists at a higher level to inherit due to their death, disqualification from receiving the estate, deprivation of the right to receive the estate, or disclaimer of the estate.
