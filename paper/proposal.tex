\begin{abstract}
We re-target the incremental legal knowledge-base (KB) pipeline from Civil-Code
Inheritance Law (BLDS~2015 Part~IV) to \textbf{Labor Law} (B\`o lu\^at Lao \dj\`ong
45/2019/QH14) and its authentic statutory update chain --- implementing decrees
(Ngh\dj\`inh \dj\njh 135/2020, 145/2020, 152/2020, 12/2022, 219/2025) and circulars
(Th\^ong t\r u). The motivation is to replace synthetic amendment fixtures with
real normative instruments, exposing deep nested exceptions (unilateral termination of
labor contracts, disciplinary dismissal, overtime limits, severance and retirement
conditions) that rigorously exercise three research primitives: (i)~incremental KB
update ($S_{upd}$), (ii)~broken-link detection ($S_{scan}$), and (iii)~defeasible
reasoning compiled to \textsc{s(CASP)} ($S_{infer}$).
\end{abstract}

\section{Architecture}
The pipeline decomposes into seven stages, each deterministic unless an optional LLM
back-end is configured (an unconfigured LLM produces an empty, ``never guess'' result):

\begin{enumerate}
  \itemsep0pt
  \item $S_{upd}$ --- versioning/amendment application over directive grammars R1--R6.
  \item $S_{know}$ --- proposition/predicate/rule extraction (concept $\sqcup$ input predicates); malformed payloads land in quarantine $Q$.
  \item $S_{infer}$ --- compilation of the in-force program $\Pi_\tau$ to \textsc{s(CASP)} with negation-as-failure blocking:
        $\mathit{blocked}(U) \leftarrow \mathit{exception\_of}(\_,U)$, $\mathit{holds}(U) \leftarrow \mathit{in\_force}(U), \neg \mathit{blocked}(U)$.
  \item $V_{legal}$ --- deterministic symbolic verifier: reward $R_{sym}$, state distance $d_{state}(s,s')=|s\,\triangle\,s'|$, and detailed feedback with penalty weights $\alpha_{precond},\alpha_{effect},\alpha_{goal}$.
  \item $S_{cot}$ --- concept-bounded legal logical chain-of-thought extractor over state transitions $\langle s_{i-1}, a_i, s_i\rangle$ at exception depths $d\in\{1,2,3\}$.
  \item $S_{scan}$ --- graph scanner reporting five error families: \emph{dead\_rule}, \emph{duplicate}, \emph{broken\_link}, \emph{blocking\_cycle}, \emph{contradiction}.
  \item $S_{repair}$ --- verifier-guided $\eta$-iteration repair loop ($\eta\in\{1,2,3\}$).
\end{enumerate}

\section{Concept Book (Labor Law)}
The concept book bounds the atoms a reasoning chain may introduce. Base \emph{input}
predicates encode facts (contract type, pregnancy, harassment, retirement age, tenure);
\emph{concept} predicates encode statute-anchored legal notions. Anchors: Articles
35 (worker unilateral termination), 36 (employer unilateral termination), 37 (prohibited
unilateral termination), 39 (unlawful termination), 46 (severance allowance), 169
(retirement age). Exception-depth hints: $d{=}1$ base rule, $d{=}2$ exception, $d{=}3$
nested exception (e.g.\ Article~39 defines unlawful termination by reference to
Articles 35--37; Article~46 carries a \emph{``tr\r u tr\r u\`ong h\dj p''} exception to
its own severance rule).

\section{Evaluation}
The evaluation dataset ($\texttt{data/labor/eval\_nested\_exceptions.jsonl}$) pairs
nested-exception cases at $d\in\{1,2,3\}$ with counterfactual pairs and
out-of-scope/quarantined items, each annotated with one of four verdicts on the
extraction/abstention surface: \texttt{Concluded}, \texttt{Refuted},
\texttt{Insufficient Basis}, \texttt{Quarantined/Out-of-Scope}. Defeasible-reasoning
accuracy is scored by $R_{sym}$; selective abstention is calibrated by the
extraction-vs-abstention trade-off, with multi-seed significance testing
(Benjamini--Hochberg FDR $q<0.05$).

The first benchmark run (\texttt{results/labor\_eval\_report.json}) scores the
deterministic symbolic ceiling at $14/14 = 100\%$ (Concluded $6/6$, Refuted $3/3$,
Insufficient Basis $2/2$, Quarantined/Out-of-Scope $3/3$), with per-depth accuracy
$8/8$ at $d{=}1$, $3/3$ at $d{=}2$, $2/2$ at $d{=}3$, and $1/1$ consistent
counterfactual pair.

\section{Status}
Base law and decrees are ingested through the registry (\texttt{src/law\_registry.py});
the curated labor sample parses to $45$ units and $226$ triples with $0$ parse errors,
$8$ nested \texttt{exception\_of} edges and $7$ condition nodes are extracted, and
$S_{scan}$ reports $0$ errors across all five families. The A2b reference grammar now
parses enumerated self-referential point-lists ($19$ \texttt{refers\_to} edges),
unblocking graph-merge/validation on the labor sample. Live s(CASP) inference runs via
the user-space SWI-Prolog pack \texttt{scasp}~1.1.4 (\texttt{library(scasp)}, no sudo);
\texttt{src/inference.py} returns raw models with justification trees over
\texttt{a9\_lld2019\_labor.pl} ($45$ in-force units), where \texttt{holds/1} is defeated
by negation-as-failure on \texttt{exception\_of}. The Phase-2 temporal amendment
(ND~135/2020 over Article~169) splits the half-open intervals
[\texttt{effective\_from},\texttt{effective\_to}) correctly: prior versions close at
\texttt{2021-01-01}, new versions open with \texttt{previous\_version}$=1$, and the
affected set captures dependent referers (\texttt{D169.K3}, \texttt{D169.K4}).
